Original text

p. 181 · extract

Sea cows were very numerous about the coast of Kamtshatka, and the Aleutan islands1, at the time when they were first discovered; but the last of this species was killed in 1768 on Bering’s island, and none have been ever seen since.

Note

  1. 01

    Sauer gives no source for the 1768 date, which he reports at second hand. Steller observed living animals only at the Commander Islands. Nordenskiöld (1881, II, p. 275 n.) doubted that the species had occurred in the Aleutians. ↩
