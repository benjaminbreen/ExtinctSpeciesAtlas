The record

Three accounts · c. 52 BCE–1549

Julius Caesar De bello Gallico

01

“These are a little below the elephant in size, and of the appearance, color, and shape of a bull.”

Caesar, Gallic War 6.28; English, 1872, p. 154

Caesar reports this at second hand, in a digression whose authenticity has been questioned.

PlaceHercynian forest, Germania · approximate
SubjectSize, hunting in pits, drinking horns
LanguageLatin · English translation

Sigismund von Herberstein Rerum Moscoviticarum commentarii

03

“Masovia, which borders on Lithuania, is the only province which has in it the kind of buffalo which in the language of the country is called thur” […]

Herberstein, 1549; trans. R. H. Major, 1852, II, p. 96

Major translates the Latin bubalus as “buffalo.”

PlaceMasovia, Poland
SubjectRoyal preserves, crossbreeding, hide girdles
