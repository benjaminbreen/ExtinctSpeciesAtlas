01 / SummaryWikipedia ↗

The great auk was a flightless North Atlantic seabird. In 1844, two birds were captured on Eldey, Iceland.

Summary adapted from Wikipedia contributors, Great auk, accessed 28 September 2026 (CC BY-SA 4.0). Image: Ole Worm, Museum Wormianum (1655), public domain, via Wikimedia Commons.
