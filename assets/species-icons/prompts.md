# Imagegen prompts

The three contact sheets were generated separately with the built-in imagegen tool. These are the final prompts. No historical image was supplied as a visual input.

## Sheet 01

> Use case: scientific-educational. Asset type: archival engraving icon contact sheet for a minimalist history-of-science website. Create one high-resolution square PNG contact sheet with a genuinely transparent alpha background. EXACT 2 by 2 grid of FOUR separate black-ink natural-history engravings, one subject fully isolated and centered in each equal quadrant, with generous empty transparent margin; do not let any subject cross quadrant boundaries. No background, no paper texture, no frame, no grid lines, no words, numbers, labels, shadows, or color. Delicate yet crisp 17th–19th-century woodcut/copperplate linework with controlled hatching, readable at 100-pixel display size. Subjects and historical visual models, strictly in this order: TOP LEFT: silphium-like Cyrenaic laserpitium, single upright umbellifer stalk with compound umbels, deeply divided leaves and robust root, visually informed by the 1644 Bodaeus van Stapel Theophrastus laserpitium woodcut; do not draw a daisy. TOP RIGHT: aurochs, massive wild bovine in three-quarter side profile, long forward-curving horns and high shoulders, visually informed by early modern European aurochs engravings such as Herberstein's. BOTTOM LEFT: dodo, stout flightless pigeon, broad hooked bill, small wings, tufted tail, stout legs, in profile, visually informed by 17th-century Dutch natural-history drawings. BOTTOM RIGHT: Rodrigues solitaire, tall flightless pigeon with long neck and long legs, small wings and hooked bill, in profile, visually informed by François Leguat's 1708 engraved plate. All four anatomically distinct and individually composed like specimens on an archival plate. Preserve actual transparency.
